London, Barcelona, Lisbon, and back to London for New Year

Mum, Dad and sister fly Kuala Lumpur to London and home again with Qatar Airways. Those four flights are fixed. Everything between them is a suggested plan built around Christmas closures and New Year's Eve in London. The London hotel for the first two nights is booked; nothing in Spain or Portugal is booked yet.

  • 08:00Land at Heathrow Terminal 4. Allow about an hour for the border and bags.

Before you fly

UK entry

Malaysian passport holders need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation before boarding. Apply in the UK ETA app or on gov.uk, one per traveller, children included.

It lasts two years and allows repeat visits, so the same ETA covers coming back from Lisbon on 30 December. A decision usually takes up to three working days.

Spain and Portugal entry

Malaysians travel visa-free for short stays. The first Schengen border (Barcelona) takes fingerprints and a photo, so allow extra time on arrival.

The EU's ETIAS travel authorisation was not yet in operation when this was checked. Look at the official EU travel site again in early December.

Luggage

The Qatar allowance does not carry over to the three European flights. Those usually include one 23 kg bag at most, and low-cost fares charge for any checked bag.

Leave the big suitcases in London on 22 December and take cabin-size bags to Spain and Portugal.

Clocks

London and Lisbon are 8 hours behind Kuala Lumpur. Barcelona is 7 hours behind.

Carry passports on every flight. London to Barcelona and Lisbon to London both cross a border.
